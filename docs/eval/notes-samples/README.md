## Takeaways

- The structural guarantees (parsing, anchors, fences) hold with a small local model because they are enforced in code, not trusted to the model.
- Factual errors inside the prose cannot be caught this way. This is measured properly by the faithfulness evaluation (#44), and a stronger model (`LLM_PROVIDER=openai`) is expected to reduce it.
- Speed: about 1 minute per 10 minutes of lecture for a single map call plus reduce on a laptop; a 4-hour lecture needs ≈ 25 map calls, i.e. several minutes in a background job (#27).
